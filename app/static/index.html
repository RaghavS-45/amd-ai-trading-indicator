Every experiment

Each run was judged against a pass rule fixed before it ran. Direction runs: accuracy (± day-clustered standard error) and mean profit per trade after costs in basis points (1 bp = 0.01%). Move-size runs: QLIKE loss (lower is better), R² and how often the price stayed inside the 80% range. “Fresh” = 2023–2024, years no design choice had seen.

How to read the indicator

Two minutes to read: what each number means, and when not to trust it.

What it answers

Every minute, after a 1-minute candle closes, the indicator answers one question: how far is AMD likely to move in the next 10 minutes? It also gives a weak hint about the direction, and shows what the market and the news are doing.

It does not tell you to buy or sell. It is a study tool for paper trading.

The numbers

Typical move
Half of 10-minute moves are smaller than this, half are bigger. “±$1.80” means a normal move from here is about $1.80 either way.
80% range
The price 10 minutes later should land inside this range about 8 times out of 10. On the chart it is the dashed band, drawn ten minutes ahead of each candle. It is the forecast move size times a multiplier measured on past data, with a slightly larger multiplier in the first and last hour, when surprises are more common.
Activity
How busy the market is compared with a normal day at the same time: calm, normal or busy. “Bigger moves than usual are likely” appears when it is far above normal.
Direction
▲ UP or ▼ DOWN with a confidence, shown only when the model leans clearly one way (about 1 signal in 5 on a normal day; the bar is re-set each morning from the previous 20 sessions); otherwise “No clear signal”. Tested on 2023–2026 data, these calls were right about 50% of the time: treat them as a weak hint.
Price levels
Prices traders watch: VWAP (today's average price weighted by volume, here IEX volume only), today's high and low so far, and yesterday's close, high and low. Each is marked inside range if it lies inside the 80% range, i.e. the price could well be there in 10 minutes. Every point on these lines uses only the minutes before it.
Context
What QQQ (big tech) and NVDA (a chip peer) did in the last 10 minutes, how many AMD headlines came out, and how many recent minutes had no trades on the free data feed.

When not to trust it

  • The first half hour. Forecasts start at 10:03 New York time (– your time): the opening minutes are too wild and some inputs need 30 minutes of data.
  • Earnings and big news. Moves are far larger than usual and the range is missed more often (5 Nov 2026: 73% inside the 80% range).
  • Many quiet minutes. The free IEX feed sees only part of the market. If “minutes without IEX trades” is high, the chart is less reliable.
  • Direction alone. No direction model we tested beat always guessing “Up” in an honest test. Never act on the arrow by itself.

Why you can trust the record

  • No repainting. A signal is made only after its candle closes, written once to a database that refuses edits, and never changed.
  • Late signals are marked. If the app was down and catches up, those signals are labelled late and not counted in the live score.
  • Outside copy. Every 5 minutes the record is also saved to a separate, dated store.
  • Same code as testing. The live app uses the exact feature code and the frozen model that were tested on training data only (up to 30 June 2026).

Market hours

New York 9:30 am – 4:00 pm, Monday to Friday (– – – your time). Outside these hours the Live page shows the last session, recomputed after the close and marked REVIEW.

The models, in one line each

Move size: our LightGBM model (gradient-boosted trees) on 15 inputs: how jumpy AMD, QQQ and NVDA were recently, AMD news count, volume and trade surprises, and the time of day. Used since 14 Nov 2026.

Baseline and backup: HAR model (Corsi, 2009) adapted to 10 minutes: a weighted mix of how jumpy AMD was recently. It is shown beside each forecast and takes over if a LightGBM input is missing.

Direction: logistic regression on 32 price, market and intraday inputs. Data: Alpaca's free IEX feed, 1-minute bars.
